By using the Site, you consent to the data practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

- Any messages, questions, or feedback you send us

**Information collected automatically:**
- Device and browser information (type, version, operating system)
- IP address and general location (country/region only)
- Pages viewed, time spent, and referring URL
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies used by our hosting platforms (Stan.store, analytics providers, social-media platforms)

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Process and fulfill your purchases
- Deliver digital products you have purchased
- Send transactional emails (receipts, product delivery, account notices)
- Send marketing emails (only if you have opted in)
- Respond to inquiries, support requests, and comments
- Improve the Site, our products, and our services
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Use
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA / UK Residents)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases:
- **Contract:** to process orders and deliver products you have requested
- **Legitimate interests:** to improve the Site, prevent fraud, and communicate with customers

We do not sell your personal information.
