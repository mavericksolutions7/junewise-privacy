The Rider

Everything else you need

Hitting the town, three ways

  • Stay in Germantown. Walk out of Pelato and keep walking. Mother's Ruin, Germantown Café, the patios. Easiest night, no cars, everyone stays together.
  • Go to Broadway. Ten minutes south. Multi-story honky-tonks, live bands on every floor, free cover. Loud, packed, and genuinely fun once — Labor Day weekend will be at full tilt.
  • Split it. Printers Alley and the downtown rooftops sit between the two. More bar, less bachelorette party.

Dress code

  • Early September in Nashville is hot and humid — plan for upper 80s to low 90s and check the forecast the week of.
  • Nothing on this itinerary requires more than a nice shirt. Pelato is a nice-shirt place, not a jacket place.
  • Broadway is concrete and standing. Real shoes.
  • Golfers: check the course dress code — collared shirt is the safe assumption.

Getting around

  • Germantown to downtown is a 5–10 minute ride. Rideshare is the move on Saturday night — downtown parking on a holiday weekend is a bad time.
  • Everything Friday and Saturday evening is walkable from the house. Only Sunday golf genuinely needs cars.
  • Nashville airport to Germantown is roughly 20 minutes without traffic.

Booking order of operations

  • Done: Pelato at 5 PM for seven, and Franklin Bridge at 9 AM Sunday. Both confirmed.
  • Now: the Sensa padel court(s) for Friday afternoon — Will's to make. Four per court, so count heads before you call.
  • Now: the Dinkville court for Saturday noon — app, website or (615) 910-3449. Labor Day weekend downtown, so sooner is better.
  • Sat AM: cookout groceries and ice during the morning walk, so Sunday is hands-off.
  • No booking needed: Jack Brown's. Just show up and be prepared to wait.

House notes

  • Two people are working Friday. Erin gets the door that shuts; Sam needs a desk until 1.
  • Late arrivals Thursday: keys, lights, and the wifi password sorted before 10 PM.
  • Bruce lives here. If you'd rather he didn't sleep on your bed, shut your door — otherwise he will take that as an invitation.
  • Two airport runs, both E: 9:30 AM Thursday and 4:15 PM Monday. Everyone else drives.
  • Sunday is the only early alarm all weekend. It's a real one.

Padel is not pickleball

  • You're playing both, on back-to-back days, and they are genuinely different sports.
  • Padel (Friday, Sensa): doubles only, in a glass-walled court. The walls are in play, like squash. Solid stringless racquet, underarm serve. Easy to pick up, hard to put down.
  • Pickleball (Saturday, Dinkville): open court, low net, perforated plastic ball, flat paddle. No walls, no squash.
  • Court shoes work for both. Neither one needs you to own anything — both venues sort you out.

Also happening in town

  • Labor Day weekend is one of the busiest of the Nashville year — book anything you actually care about.
  • Deep Tropics (music, art and style festival) and Nashville Dance Fest both run Sept 4–6 if anyone wants a wildcard afternoon.
  • The Tennessee State Museum by the Capitol Mall is free and air-conditioned, which is a better combination than it sounds.
